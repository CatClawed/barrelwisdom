There are five seeds in the game. Growing these in the garden at the hideout will result in items that are between 50 and 100% of the seed’s quality. Traits do not transfer; they are random as ever. This is useful for farming elemental cores, as well as getting regular materials with spread effects on them. For a list of what I’ve seen grow…
