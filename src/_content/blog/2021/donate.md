## About

Barrel Wisdom is a passion project. I am the sole developer of this site and the primary contributor. I genuinely enjoy making game guides and foresee myself doing it for a while yet. Quite frankly, this site is rather nice for something an amateur slapped together, but it can be improved, with your help.

As it is currently, the website is funded primarily through donations, with extra costs coming out of my own pocket. The costs are not (currently) ridiculous, but any amount you can spare would help a lot.

Current costs include:

* Domain name
* Server
* Image hosting
* Sanity

Of course, donations do help cover those essentials. But they can also help me improve the site. In particular, I'd like to get a better logo and some art professionally done.

If you can't afford to donate that's quite alright with me, but I'd appreciate it if you would spread the word about this site wherever you go. It actually does help the site gain traffic.
