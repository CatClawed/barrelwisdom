<h2>Part 0: About</h2>
<p>Hi, I&rsquo;m angela, and I&rsquo;m putting this guide together as what I consider a simple, foolproof way to clear all of the story bosses in Atelier Lulua. It&rsquo;s not meant as an &ldquo;ultimate set&rdquo; or anything of the sort, it&rsquo;s based around a specific strategy and the gear is simplified down to a level where it should be easily reproducible and fairly easy to follow. Hopefully that is actually the case!</p>

This guide is intended for bosses from chapter 9 onward, and will work for all of them very easily.

Feel free to leave comments if any part is confusing!

<h2>Part 2: Gathering</h2>
<p>Most of the gathering for your equipment will be done at <strong>Fire&rsquo;s Domain: Deep Depths</strong>. You can get a lot of high-tier traits and materials here, so it&rsquo;s the perfect spot.</p>
<p>You can see the gathering route you should take here (link is timestamped): <a href="https://youtu.be/yYWhqlt9F5o?t=18482">(World Record 5/19/2020) Atelier Lulua CHARISMA in 6:27:47</a></p>

<p>Run this route 3-5 times and you should have everything you need; if you find out you&rsquo;re missing something while synthesizing then just come back and gather some more.</p>

### Money

<p>Quite a bit of Cole is required for this (25-30k or so?), and that&rsquo;s on the cheap end for gear. If you find yourself running short on money, then go and do requests. Mostly gathering and synthesis requests - combat requests aren&rsquo;t worth it except for the one to kill like, Golden Puni.</p>
<p>Once you&rsquo;re out of requests you can turn in, simply go back to the Atelier and sleep for 10 days. This will refresh them. I usually only bother turning in requests I already have the materials for.</p>
<p>Requests get more annoying after completing B-rank promotion, so if you haven&rsquo;t done it yet, don&rsquo;t. You can still get the good easy ones if you have, they&rsquo;re just more rare.</p>
<p>I recommend registering a few items to get the most out of your request turn-ins:</p>
<ul>
<li>Quality 48+ Nectar</li>
<li>Quality 73+ Bomb</li>
<li>Quality 40+ Lightning Bomb</li>
</ul>
<p>These are requested fairly often. The best turn in is by far the Nectar. You get 2500 cole each time.</p>
<p>It doesn&rsquo;t matter which city you do requests at.</p>
<h2>Part 3: Synthesis</h2>
<p>Alright, now that we&rsquo;ve finished prep - time to put it all together! I listed the most important materials in the Gathering section, however many items will require other materials that you probably already have or are trivial to acquire. If you don&rsquo;t remember where to get a certain material, you can usually find it in a shop or the gather location will be listed in the in-game encyclopedia.</p>
<p>For each item, I will only list the <em>important</em> materials. If a material isn&rsquo;t listed, that means you can put whatever you want there. Also, order matters! You <strong>must</strong> add materials in the order listed to get the proper awakened effects. When it matters, I will list the awakened effects just to be safe, so that you can easily double check whether or not you did it right. You can see the awakened effects at the bottom right corner.</p>
<p>Before starting this section, make sure you are at least <strong>Alchemy Level 40</strong>, otherwise some traits may not be transferable. You need to be at least level 43 to finish the game anyways, so it&rsquo;s not really an unnecessary grind if you aren&rsquo;t there yet.</p>
<p>And remember, if you can&rsquo;t find any of the traits on the materials listed, go back to gather at Fire&rsquo;s Domain until you get them. All of the traits you will need come from there.</p>
<p>Also, you may want to save before starting. Just in case.</p>

<h2>Part 4: Strategy</h2>
<p>So the items are finished! Now how does this all work? Well, first thing first, let&rsquo;s set up the party.</p>
<p><strong>Formation:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Front Row: </strong>Sterk, Rorona, Aurel</li>
<li><strong>Back Row: </strong>Lulua, Piana</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Equipment:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong><strong>Lulua:</strong></strong></li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Interrupt: </strong>Healing Bell</li>
<li>Rest doesn&rsquo;t matter</li>
</ul>
<li><strong><strong>Piana:</strong></strong></li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Interrupt: </strong>Healing Bell</li>
<li>Rest doesn&rsquo;t matter</li>
</ul>
<li><strong><strong>Rorona:</strong></strong></li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Interrupt: </strong>Healing Bell</li>
<li>Un-equip EVERYTHING - Rorona should not have equipment. Yes, Rorona will die. A lot. This is intentional.</li>
<li>Exception: if you made the optional items (Brave Sigil and Spirit Necklace), then equip them as her accessories. These help her die more.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong><strong>Sterk:&nbsp;</strong></strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Equip all of the gear you made</li>
</ul>
<li><strong><strong>Aurel:</strong></strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Equip all of the gear you made</li>
</ul>
<li><strong><strong>Tools:</strong></strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Healing Bells</li>
<li>Mystical Ankh if you made it</li>
</ul>
</ul>
<p>If your characters aren&rsquo;t level 50, it&rsquo;s easier if you grind that out first. I don&rsquo;t know where the best spot to level is. The bosses are still doable at lower levels, but level 50 unlocks ultimate attacks which do a lot of damage.</p>
<p>Now, for the actual combat strategy:</p>
<p>In general, your party will be at low HP almost the entire time with this set. <em>This is okay.</em> For the most part, Sterk and Aurel won&rsquo;t die very often (they will survive with 1HP due to the amount of Avoid KO Stacking).</p>
<p>Use Interrupt Healing bells when one of these conditions is met:</p>
<ul>
<li>Anyone dies</li>
<li>Sterk or Aurel is at 1HP</li>
<li>You need to cleanse debuffs</li>
</ul>
<p>Don&rsquo;t use them otherwise really, it&rsquo;s unnecessary. You can also use healing bells manually with Rorona to make sure the interrupts are up when you need them.</p>
<p>Now, what to do for each character&rsquo;s turn:</p>
<p><strong>Sterk: </strong>Use skill &ldquo;Astral Caster&rdquo;</p>
<p><strong>Rorona: </strong>Attack, Healing Bell if you need to. She can throw bombs instead of attacking if you have them (they aren&rsquo;t covered in this guide). You can use her Ultimate Skill <strong>Spinning Synthesis Classroom</strong> if you need to recover Sterk/Aurel MP.</p>
<p>If you made a Mystical Ankh, you can use it on Aurel/Sterk if they ever get stunned to break them out of it.</p>
<p><strong>Aurel: </strong>Most turns you will use normal attacks. Use his skill &ldquo;Resting Spirit Stance&rdquo; before using his ultimate. When your ultimate gauge is full (it will fill up quickly!), then use his ultimate attack skill <strong>Scorching Heavy Snow.&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p>If you get unlucky and your entire front row dies at the same time, it&rsquo;s possible to recover. Keep in mind that this is an uncommon occurrence - if it does happen, it just means you got unlucky on that attempt and oh well. You can use healing bell interrupts to revive your back and front rows, then switch lulua and piana out once everyone is revived. Or if you die just go back and try again I guess. This likely will not happen but it is possible, so just putting a warning for that here.</p>
