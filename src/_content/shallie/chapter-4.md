<p>Shallie finally meets Shallie.
</p>
<ul><li> <i>Lotte</i>
<ul><li> If you need Gravistones, go to <a href="/shallie/locations/baskes-region/en#filled-cave">Filled Cave</a>. Stera is awful impressed with my crap synthesis techniques.</li>
<li> A scene with Miruca will unlock a life task about not eating. Just don't feed Lotte any <a href="/shallie/categories/food">(Food)</a> for a while. (Make sure you've used food items a few times to trip this.)</li></ul></li></ul>
<dl><dd></dd></dl>
<ul><li> <i>Stera</i>
<ul><li> A scene between Stera and Kortes about exercise will unlock a life task. You can finish this immediately; just keep jumping.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>At the Hunter's Hangout, you'll be introduced to mining. Both Shallies can do this, though it's not particularly rewarding.
</p><p>You'll get a special request from Raoul with a (!) next to it. Talking about it reveals that it's a <a href="/shallie/items/stellapearl">Stellapearl</a> for Solle.
This is the first of its kind for Lotte; not so much for Stera.
</p><p>...Unfortunately, there's really nothing to do in this chapter. No new areas, but you may choose to fight some bosses (I'll have
more on that next section). Just follow the life tasks and all is well. I'd suggest making sure you're around ADV level 25, and
Alch Level 35. You have the beginning of the next chapter to do this stuff too, so no rush.
</p><p>To start off the chapter, however, you must be above alchemy level 20. You'll FINALLY unlock the ability to transfer ingredient properties
to the resulting item. I'd suggest just playing around with it for now, if you're new to the series. In particular, be aware that PP, which
is what you consume to put properties on items, can be boosted via other properties.
</p>
