<ul><li> <i>Areas Unlocked:</i> <a href="/shallie/locations/stellard-southern-archipelago/en#seat-of-eternity">Seat of Eternity</a></li>
<li> <i>Plus Note</i>
<ul><li> Be extra sure to have seen all the Seed-related events.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>Before I go on, if you're on Stera's side be sure to have a Talisman of Ancient Beasts with you. You'll get extra dialogue
with this boss.
</p><p>Go to Geofront. Instead of going toward the warp, go straight to the area that used to be a dead end. You'll run into a boss fight worth a trophy.
If you lose, he's gone forever, so beware. He's best beaten on a second run, but you can do it on the easiest difficulty, at
a decent level, with pretty mediocre (stat boosting) equipment. I know because I did it.
</p><p>Here's my super duper cheap way of beating the King of Ancient Beasts. Level 55. Story Watcher difficulty.
This is for those of you who want the game to end ASAP.
</p><p>Lead off with Kortes in the back, possibly Jurie as well. Do not use Wilbell's Volcano skill.
Make sure you get 110% when you activate burst, and pick Kortes and/or Jurie. Preferably, you can
swap someone out to get 120+ for both. On top of that, I had a Lava Cube with <a href="/shallie/properties/Carries-Heat">Carries Heat</a>
going. But that's about it. He went down.
</p><p>My equipment? The stuff I made in the chapter 8. Zero changes. But to give a full rundown... (it isn't that special):
</p>
<ul><li> Gunade Ring with Herculean Strength/Divine Protection, but just Speed</li>
<li> Dragon Shield with More Explosive Power / Fighting Spirit S / Tranquil Spirit</li>
<li> Weapons with Enhance Burst, Enhance Item (when applicable), Enhance Skill. Enhance Crit was emphasized, but not everywhere.</li>
<li> Armor had DMG Cut, kind of a crapshoot after that. Wilbell/Jurie had Critical.</li>
<li> Everything had Attack/Speed/Defense Huge Enhance.</li>
<li> Basically, put a lot into burst damage bonuses and kill him within a single burst.</li>
<li> If you don't manage to kill him in burst, he wins. Seriously. I don't care if he has 1000 health left (I've been there).</li>
<li> Oooor you can make "real" equipment and beat him the hard way. (Or just grind to level 99 and beat him anyway on Hardcore.)</li></ul>
<p><i>Beyond the Beast King is the path to the final dungeon</i>, as well as the DLC part of Geofront. There's nothing special to add, honestly.
Go forth and beat the tar out of the final boss.
</p><p>So go ahead and read up on <a href="/shallie/preparation-and-farming">this page</a> if you want to beat bosses the hard way. There is a tooooon of good stuff in there.
</p>
<ul><li> Plus Version Bosses (NO HOPE HP values listed)
<ul><li> Coral Dragon (600,000 HP) will appear after beating Fran Pfeil, along the same route.</li>
<li> Ruler of Beast Kings (500,000 HP) is in the "DLC Area", The Abyss. You should be able to sneak around him for the recipe book beyond him.</li>
<li> As with the PS3 version, the Controller (500,000 HP) is at the end of that area</li>
<li> After beating the Controller, Omnipotent (750,000 HP) appears in the same spot when you return.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>You'll get to choose which end you take, so make a save after beating the final boss to avoid some of the blah blah blah.
</p>
<ul><li> End requirements
<ul><li> Lotte/Stera generics, automatically given</li>
<li> Make the Flying Board (Lotte).</li>
<li> Stera's ending seems tied to doing life tasks (and a Solle event telling you how great you are). You'll get a special event with Solle about this. I did 103 non-main tasks on my NG+ run and got it.</li>
<li> For the NG+ end, just fulfill the non-generic requirements.</li>
<li> In Plus, if you did all the Seed-related events, you get an extension apparently. (I haven't seen the non-extended version.) This also seems to be tied to getting the NG+ exclusive end.</li></ul></li></ul>
