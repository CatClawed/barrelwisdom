* (Wealth + Chim ends)
* After Chim's birth and hitting Platinum rank, there will be an event with Iksel. You'll get [Iksel Recipe Book](totori/recipe-books/iksel-recipe-book).
* Make the items, hand them to Iksy. He'll give you [Awesome Slow Food](totori/recipe-books/awesome-slow-food). Turn the items in for [Great Ingredients](totori/recipe-books/great-ingredients). Make the new item (and don't make it crappy; Iksy will reject it) and to Iksy for [Totori's Cooking](totori/recipe-books/totori-s-cooking).
* Make '''that''' item, over 80 quality so you don't have to make it twice. Iksy will challenge you to a cook-off, whatever Atelier you happen to be at (stalker!). Bring the Brunch to him. ''You must win.'' You'll be rewarded with a Water of Life and [Head Chef's Gift](totori/recipe-books/head-chef-s-gift).
* After, there's an event with Iksel. Rorona calls him a meanie.
* By the way, you need 500,000 Cole for the Wealth end. See [Money](totori/money) for the best (if utterly boring) way.
