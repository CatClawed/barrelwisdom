<ul><li> Area
<ul><li> <a href="/escha/locations/dragon-s-nest/en">Dragon's Nest</a></li>
<li> Get a <a href="/escha/items/diamond-gemstone/en">Diamond Gemstone</a> before leaving.</li>
<li> You should get the final lithograph piece here.</li>
<li> See <a href="/escha/locations/southern-ruins/en#ruins-altar">Ruins Altar</a> and <a href="/escha/locations/southern-ruins/en#crumbling-altar">Crumbling Altar</a>.</li>
<li> If you've made the items I suggested up till now you will have no trouble with the boss. Otherwise, get Escha &amp; Logy in the party and try Double Draw, with your best item last and with Threia's assist debuff. One thing, though. The boss does have little missiles he likes to launch at you. They're a timed effect and you can't defend against them, so make sure you're at decent health when he launches 'em.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>In the Administration Office, you get a plot related event about a metal eating dragon, which is followed up with an event at the Dragon's Fist Tavern and
another at the Hall (you get <a href="/escha/recipe-books/dragon-repellant/en">Dragon Repellant</a>). Long story short, they're going to suggest that you scare it away. Well, if you want to do everything,
you're going to have to kill it, and you might as well kill it sooner than later. You are very capable of doing it during this assignment. Basically, this
dragon is the prerequisite to fighting a stronger dragon at the end of the game. For the record, I forgot to equip Escha with attacking items, threw her in
my party (she was useless!) and STILL beat the thing. It's not as hard as the game makes it out to be, but I still suggest being more prepared than I was.
</p><p>With Clone, you'll get the next chapter of her story. Finally, you'll get an event with Nio. If you haven't played Ayesha, uh, go play it now. I'll wait.&#160;:P
Seriously, all the scenes with Nio tug at the heartstrings.
</p>

 <h4>Synthing</h4>
<p>Properties are all yours. Use Effect and Quantity as you like.
</p>
<ul><li> <a href="/escha/items/blitz-stone/en">Blitz Stone</a>
<ul><li> Ingredients: Corundum Stone, Capacitive Paper, Mixed Lubricant (or Black Powder), Black Neutralizer</li>
<li> Toss in Corundum Stone, Capacitive Paper, Mixed Lubricant.</li>
<li> Use Power+ LV1 on Black Neutralizer, throw it in, then use Division on the Neutralizer.</li>
<li> Use Conversion LV1 on Wind, then Reset Attribute on Fire.</li>
<li> Conversion LV2 on Fire, then Draw Property. This gets you the LV5 Property.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li> <a href="/escha/items/airto-blitz/en">Airto Blitz</a>
<ul><li> Ingredients: Dragon Tail, Iron Plate, Rough Disk, Black Neutralizer</li>
<li> Throw in Dragon Tail, Iron Plate, and Black Neutralizer.</li>
<li> Use Conversion LV2 on Fire, then Power+ LV2 on Rough Disk.</li>
<li> Use Conversion LV1 on Fire, then Strengthen Material on Rough Disk.</li>
<li> Throw in Rough Disk, then use Division on it. Use Reset Attribute on Wind.</li></ul></li></ul>
