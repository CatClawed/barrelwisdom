<p>You know the drill. Trip all the events you see. You should continue the book with Clone and get a plot event with Nio.
</p><p>Note that Nio has a Dunkelheit. It's expensive, but it's also a really good material. You can, however, gather these for free at <a href="/escha/locations/lab-of-eternity/en">Lab of Eternity</a> and
<a href="/escha/locations/southern-ruins/en">Southern Ruins</a>. You will need a Plant Nutrients. And note that it's rare; if you want to farm them, you'll want to save first.
</p><p>Before you leave, make the airship parts. You can do it after buying the new recipe from Nio, especially if you followed my instructions and got a
<a href="/escha/items/diamond-gemstone/en">Diamond Gemstone</a>. The big reason you want to do this is because you will travel faster through the air.
</p><p>Yeah, this section is pretty empty. The fun stuff starts in the next assignment, so, uh, enjoy the break?
