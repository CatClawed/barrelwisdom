<h2>Goal of the Game</h2>
<p>To acquire the trophy, you need to get Nekura no Miko. To do this (untested instructions!):
</p>
<ul><li> Focus on either Health/Charm or Wits/Charm.</li>
<li> Stress only affects what questions you get. It's perfectly acceptable to have 5 stress at any point.</li>
<li> Press square to change the question as necessary.</li>
<li> Have exactly 5 in Health/Wits and 5 in Charm at the fourth growth.
<ul><li> Honey is the easiest monster to aim for.</li>
<li> For other monsters, ensure total Wits/Health is 20 and total Charm is 15 (regardless of whether the monster evolves to Grand/Pop at this growth). An additional turn can be taken before moving on to the next step, if necessary.</li></ul></li>
<li> Reduce Health, Wits, and Charm to 1 or 0. You should gain 10 points in your two necessary stats over the next four turns as you reduce, as long as you use the suggested question/answer combo.
<ul><li> Question: It seems mad at you. What would you like to do?</li>
<li> Answer: Make fun of it</li></ul></li>
<li> Around 8th growth, you should get a third stage monster, with Nekura no Miko following as soon as the stats are low enough.</li></ul>
