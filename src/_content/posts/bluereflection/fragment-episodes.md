<i class="fas fa-exclamation-circle"></i> means you should follow it for as many effects as possible in the database.

<i class="fas fa-star"></i> means you can only get the fragment from that choice.

<i class="fas fa-arrow-right"></i> means the effect is better from a gameplay perspective.

<i class="far fa-circle"></i> means you are guaranteed a copy of this effect.
