---
title: "Puni Raising"
pubDate: "2021-06-27"
updatedDate: "2024-11-14"
description: "Want all the punis? You need to give them specific types of items."
---

There are materials you can't get from seeds, but you can get from puni raising. I don't generally suggest doing it, as each puni has a massive loot table. But if you have a specific need for it, you can try to save scum it out. They won’t always be 999 quality (some will approach this), but you will get things like S rank Dragon Eyes, Eternal Fires, monster drops, and so on. 

You feed the puni six items over two days, then it returns a day later with a random selection of items.

To get puni with higher value stats, I like to use Eldrocode, Holy Drop, and random fillers. Don’t consider this a formal list; it’s just a couple things I’ve noted.

For puni with lower value stats, such as Abyss Puni, I make a Marblestone and 5 Poison Cubes, each with no effects and at S rank. The reason for this is that they both have the (Stone) and (Poison) categories (without any extra categories!), respectively, which tend to grant negative values to Const./Luster/Mood. To state it simply: the const./luster/mood values depend on the categories (and ranks) of the items you feed to the puni.
